Enrollment is one row per school per grade per year -- before merging, we roll it up to one row per school per year (summing enrollment across grades), then join that against Budget's one row per school per year. That keeps the merge to roughly one output row per school-year instead of multiplying every grade row by every budget line.

Years are matched by school year, not literal text -- Budget's fiscal year (e.g. FY26) is automatically shifted to the school year it funds (FY26 → 2025-2026), per CPS's own convention. School names are matched case/whitespace-insensitively, but CPS doesn't guarantee identical spelling across the two sources, so not every school will find a match -- see the match rate below.
