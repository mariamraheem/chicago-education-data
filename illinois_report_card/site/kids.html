Source: Illinois State Board of Education, Illinois Report Card Public Data Set, scraped directly from isbe.net. "CPS" is Chicago Public Schools District 299; "Illinois" is the statewide total across all districts. The Kindergarten Individual Development Survey (KIDS) rates incoming kindergartners on 14 measures across three developmental areas (social-emotional, language & literacy, and math) within the first weeks of the school year. ISBE has only published this data since 2023-24, and did not publish a statewide comparison row for CPS's first year (2023-24) -- that year is CPS-only. "Children with Disabilities" and "IEP" report identical values for CPS in every year shown here; at kindergarten entry, having an IEP is effectively what qualifies a student as a "child with a disability" for this survey, so the two populations are the same.
