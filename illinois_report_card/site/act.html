Source: Illinois State Board of Education, Illinois Report Card Public Data Set, scraped directly from isbe.net. "CPS" is Chicago Public Schools District 299; "Illinois" is the statewide total across all districts. 2019-20 has no assessment data (assessments were waived for COVID). ISBE's SAT-era files (2017-18 to 2022-23) reported only Level 1-4 performance percentages, not a single average score or proficiency rate -- ISBE didn't publish a precomputed SAT proficiency rate until 2023-24, and 2017-18 alone lacks even an average score. ISBE replaced the SAT with the ACT as the state's HS assessment starting 2024-25 -- the ELA/Math proficiency rate is shown as one trend with a dashed break at that switch (same % scale, different test); the average-score statistics are NOT comparable across that switch (SAT subject scores run roughly 200-800, ACT subject scores roughly 1-36) and are each shown only for the years their own test was given.
